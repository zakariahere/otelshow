Frame the mental model before any YAML. In the scrape world the hard part was always discovery — Prometheus has to know every target, which is why we maintain scrape configs and relabeling rules. Push inverts responsibility: the app is configured once with a local address and the delivery machinery (batching, retry, backpressure) lives in the SDK and the collector. And the punchline in the bottom-right cell: pull never carried traces or logs anyway — that's why we had Kafka topics on the side. Push through OTLP unifies all three.
