06tail sampling

Sampling: decide after the trace ends

head sampling

Decides at the first span. Cheap, stateless, done in the SDK.

But it's blind: it can't know the trace will error out or take 1.2 s. The interesting traces get dropped with the boring ones.

tail sampling

Waits for the whole trace, then judges it. Smart — and stateful: the collector buffers spans for decision_wait and holds up to num_traces in memory.

Intelligence has a RAM bill. That's the trade.
