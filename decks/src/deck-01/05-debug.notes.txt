This is the payoff of verbosity: detailed — the collector narrates everything it ingests, every five seconds. Teach the hierarchy once and every later lesson gets easier: ResourceMetrics is the WHO (one entity — our app), InstrumentationScope is HOW the data got in (the prometheus receiver, versioned), then each Metric with its Descriptor, then typed datapoints with two timestamps and a value. On /actuator/prometheus this was a flat text line: dice_rolls_total 1.0. In here it has shape. Same data — richer container. Live demo: curl /roll a few times and watch Value tick up in this log.
