What we didn't cover

  • Agent vs gateway topologies — a collector per host, per pod, or one central fleet; when each shape wins.
  • Kafka, both directions — the collector has a Kafka receiver and exporter: your existing topics can feed it, or be fed by it, during migration. The old world plugs in; it doesn't have to die first.
  • Scaling & HA — tail sampling is stateful, so load balancing needs trace-ID-aware routing in front.
  • OTTL beyond the basics — we used replace_pattern and IsMatch; the language does far more.
  • SDK-side sampling & views — the cheapest span is the one never created; the collector is the safety net, not the whole strategy.
Each of these starts from a config block you now know how to read — receivers, processors, exporters.
