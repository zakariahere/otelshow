why the detour

Tempo inside grafana/otel-lgtm binds its OTLP receivers to 127.0.0.1 only — unreachable from any other container. So our traces enter through the LGTM box's own embedded collector on :4318, which forwards to Tempo locally.

  • Not a hack — this is the standard agent → gateway topology, in miniature.
  • In production: a collector per node/pod (agent) forwards to a central collector fleet (gateway) that owns backend credentials, sampling, and egress.
Collectors compose — OTLP in, OTLP out means any collector can sit in front of any other.
