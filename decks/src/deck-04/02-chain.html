  • memory_limiter first — refuse data before the collector OOMs, not after.
  • batch last — everything upstream sees single items; the wire sees efficient chunks.
  • drop before mutate — never pay to transform a span you were about to kill.
A pipeline is not a bag of plugins — the sequence is a design decision you can get wrong.
