Kafka didn't die — it moved behind the collector

what changes

Kafka becomes a transport concern inside the pipeline — the collector ships kafka exporter and receiver when you need buffering or fan-out between hops.

what stops

Apps hand-rolling producers, serializers and topic conventions per signal. The app's contract shrank to OTLP to one address.

Keep the broker if the architecture needs it — but it's the platform team's plumbing now, invisible to every service.
