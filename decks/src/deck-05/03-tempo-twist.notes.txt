This one surprised us during setup: pointing otlp_http/tempo at a Tempo port directly fails, because Tempo in the LGTM image listens on loopback only. The fix is to send to lgtm:4318 - the all-in-one image runs its OWN collector inside, and that one can reach Tempo on localhost. Pause here: this accidental architecture is exactly how real deployments look. Sidecar/daemonset collectors close to the app (cheap, local buffering), gateway collectors at the edge (auth, tail sampling, egress control). Because both sides speak OTLP, chaining is free. If the team remembers one thing: OTLP in, OTLP out = LEGO.
