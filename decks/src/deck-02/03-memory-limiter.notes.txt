The mental model: a bouncer at the door. If the club is full you get refused at the entrance - you don't get let in and then thrown out of a window. Soft limit = limit_mib minus spike_limit_mib, that's where refusals begin (300 MiB here); hard limit 400 MiB forces GC. Key operational point: refusing data creates backpressure - an OTLP sender will retry, a scrape just gets missed. That's also why it MUST be first: any processor placed before it would still allocate memory for doomed data. If someone asks 'why not just give the container more RAM' - you'd move the cliff, not remove it.
