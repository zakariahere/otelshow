War story from building this deck: while verifying tail sampling we sent 20 requests and Tempo search showed exactly 1 trace - looked like a catastrophic sampling bug. Thirty seconds later the same query returned 7, precisely on target. Nothing was wrong except our patience. This is generic to Tempo (ingester flush interval), not to our pipeline. The operational habit: in live demos and in incident response, treat 'missing recent traces' as a freshness question first, a pipeline question second.
