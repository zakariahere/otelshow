The tour, in four stops

  1. Explore → Tempo — search otelshow-app, open a GET /play trace, spot the 1.2 s outliers in the duration column.
  2. Explore → Loki — {service_name="otelshow-app"}; open a log line and jump log → trace through its trace_id.
  3. Explore → Prometheus — http_server_request_duration_*, dice_rolls_total: push-delivered, never scraped by this Prometheus.
  4. Dashboards — the LGTM image ships JVM and RED dashboards; they are already populated with our traffic.
End every incident the same way: one trace_id stitches all three panes together.
