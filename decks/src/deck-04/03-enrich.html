Every record leaves knowing where it came from

resource_detection — environment self-awareness
resource_detection:   # 'resourcedetection' is now a
  detectors: [env, system]  # deprecated alias (0.158)
  override: false

Adds os.type, host.name, container id — the collector fills in what the app can't know it doesn't know.

attributes/team — key/value surgery
attributes/team:
  actions:
    - key: team
      value: platform-observability
      action: upsert

Runs on every span, metric and log record that passes through — ownership tagging with zero app releases.
