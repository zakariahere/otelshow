03PROCESSOR RULE #1

memory_limiter: always first

THE BOUNCER

A collector that dies of OOM loses everything in flight — every signal, every pipeline, all at once.

memory_limiter checks usage every check_interval and starts refusing new data before the process is at risk.

HOW THE LIMITS WORK

limit_mib: 400 — the hard limit. Above it, data is refused and the GC is forced.

spike_limit_mib: 100 — the soft margin. Refusals start at 400 − 100 = 300 MiB, leaving headroom for bursts.

Refusals push backpressure to the receiver — well-behaved senders (OTLP) retry with what they buffered.

It only protects what sits behind it — which is why it must be the first processor in every pipeline.
