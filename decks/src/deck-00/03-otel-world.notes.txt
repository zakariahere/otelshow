The target picture. The app pushes everything — traces, metrics AND logs — over one protocol, OTLP, to one address: an OpenTelemetry Collector running next to it. The collector fans out to Tempo for traces, Loki for logs, Prometheus for metrics. Note what changed operationally: when we swap a backend or need to scrub a field, that's collector configuration. No app releases, no library upgrades across forty services.
