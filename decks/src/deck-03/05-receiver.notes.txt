Collector side of the handshake: one otlp receiver block serves both gRPC (4317) and HTTP (4318), and it feeds three separate pipelines — one per signal — all ending in the debug exporter for now, deliberately: this session is about READING the data, shipping it to backends is lesson 5. The highlighted 0.0.0.0 is a genuine trap: since collector 0.104 the default bind is localhost, which inside a container means "only me". Your app will fail to connect and the collector log will say nothing. Also note the greyed-out scrape pipeline: still running, still feeding Prometheus, just without debug noise. We didn't break the old world to start the new one.
