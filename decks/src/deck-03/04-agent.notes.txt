This banner is the real startup log from our app container. Two lines to point at: the JVM itself confirms it picked up the agent from JAVA_TOOL_OPTIONS, then the agent announces version 2.30.0. Design point worth dwelling on: we shipped the agent jar in the image from the very first branch, dormant. That's a pattern you can adopt tomorrow — bake the jar into your base images now, and instrumenting a service later becomes a Helm values change, not a rebuild. On Java 25 you may see a sun.misc.Unsafe warning at startup — cosmetic, ignore it.
