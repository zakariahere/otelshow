filter: the cheapest span is a dead span

filter/noise:
  error_mode: ignore
  trace_conditions:
    # our own scraper hits /actuator/prometheus
    # every 5s - each hit is a span. Kill them.
    - IsMatch(span.attributes["url.path"], "/actuator.*")
  • OTTL conditions — a small expression language over spans, metrics and logs.
  • error_mode: ignore — a broken condition skips the record, it doesn't crash the pipeline.
