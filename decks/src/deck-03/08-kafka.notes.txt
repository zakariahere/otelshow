This is the question the room will ask, so answer it head-on: we are not throwing Kafka away. If we want durable buffering between an edge collector and a central gateway, the collector has first-class kafka exporter and receiver components — that pattern survives. What dies is every app owning a producer, a serializer, and knowledge of topic names per signal type. The broker becomes an implementation detail of the telemetry platform, swappable without touching a single service. That's the real value: the app's contract shrank to OTLP.
