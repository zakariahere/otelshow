03PULL VS PUSH

Pull asks around, push fires and forgets

ConcernPull (Prometheus scrape)Push (OTLP)
discoveryThe backend must FIND every app — service discovery, scrape configs, relabeling.The app fires at one preconfigured local address. Nobody hunts.
deliveryBackend's problem. App exposes an endpoint and hopes someone comes.SDK/agent owns it: batching, gRPC streams, in-band acks.
retryMissed scrape = hole in the data. Nobody retries.Exporter retries with backoff; the collector buffers downstream.
app knowsNothing — but also can't ship traces or logs this way at all.ONE address: the collector. All three signals.
Pull only ever solved metrics. Push over OTLP is the first model that carries traces, metrics and logs through the same front door.
