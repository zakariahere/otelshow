A tooling aside that pays rent forever. The debug exporter has three verbosity levels; detailed is what we've been reading. Two operational notes: one, if you find old blog posts using the "logging" exporter, that component is gone — it's "debug" now. Two, in production you don't delete the exporter definition, you just demote the verbosity or take it out of the pipeline's exporter list. When a team says "our metrics aren't arriving", wiring debug into that pipeline for five minutes usually answers whose fault it is — before anyone opens a dashboard.
