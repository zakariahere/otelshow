The prometheus receiver goes and gets the data — a scrape loop living inside the collector. The world it reads from doesn't change.

The otlp receiver waits for apps to push (that's lesson 03). Same stage, opposite direction of travel.

This branch has zero processors. Receiver → exporter is the smallest legal pipeline — everything between is opt-in.
