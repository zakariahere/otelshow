Here's the course map. Six branches, six sessions, and the discipline is that each branch changes as little as possible: when we diff a branch against the previous one, everything you see IS that session's topic — nothing else moves. We start in familiar territory: the collector scraping Prometheus endpoints like Prometheus does today. Session three is the pivot, where the app starts pushing. By six, the collector is computing metrics out of traces and making sampling decisions. You can replay any of this later — it's all in the repo.
